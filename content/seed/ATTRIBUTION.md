# Content Attribution — `content/seed/phase2/chengyu.json`

**Dataset:** Chengyu (4-character idioms) — CC-CEDICT-extracted, curated, and enriched narrative content (Epic 23, Story 23.1).

This dataset combines multiple **CC BY-SA 4.0** sources. Share-alike is inherited from the source data; our own narrative prose is original project work and is explicitly **not** a verbatim copy of any source text.

## Sources (all CC BY-SA 4.0)

### 1. CC-CEDICT (idiom string, pinyin, literal + figurative English)

- **Provider:** MDBG (`mdbg.net`)
- **Source file:** `data/CC-CEDICT/cedict_1_0_ts_utf-8_mdbg.txt`
- **Derived extract (phase-1):** `content/seed/phase1/cc-cedict-entries.json` (generated by `apps/backend/scripts/generate/cc-cedict-entries.ts`)
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Used for:** the idiom string, tone-marked pinyin, and the literal/figurative English meanings (`literalMeaning`, `figurativeMeaning`). Where CC-CEDICT had no usable `lit.`/`fig.` gloss (e.g. untagged entries like 自相矛盾 / 瓜田李下), the authoring values were supplied by this project and cross-checked against the KB and Chinese Wiktionary.

### 2. Chinese Wiktionary 詞源 (origin-story grounding)

- **Provider:** Wikimedia Foundation — `zh.wiktionary.org`
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Used for:** confirming each idiom's classical source passage (詞源) and its Wikisource citation. The narrative `story` text is **rewritten in this project's own words** (learner-friendly English) and is **not** a verbatim copy of any Wiktionary or classical passage.

### 3. zh Wikisource (primary classical texts)

- **Provider:** Wikimedia Foundation — `zh.wikisource.org`
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0 (public-domain classical texts; transcription licensed CC BY-SA 4.0)
- **Used for:** grounding every `storySource` citation (e.g. 《史记·卷七·项羽本纪》 → `zh.wikisource.org/wiki/史記/卷007`). Each `storySource` cites a known classical work from the story-23.1 IMP era table (plus the documented expansions: 新序, 乐府诗集, 牟子理惑论, 历代名画记).

## Original project work (not copyleft)

The following are **original work of this project** and are not copied from any source:

- The narrative `story` prose (authored in our own words from the verified classical event).
- The modern-usage `example` sentences and their English translations.
- The `segments` tokenization (text / pinyin / gloss / entity links).
- The `theme` taxonomy and `era` tags (new authoring decisions, per the epic BR).

Because the underlying idiom strings / meanings (CC-CEDICT) and the origin-story grounding (Wikimedia) are CC BY-SA 4.0, the **derived dataset** `chengyu.json` remains CC BY-SA 4.0 (share-alike inherited). Our narrative text is recorded here as original project work and is not a verbatim reproduction of any source.

## Restricted sources — none shipped

No text from restricted or non-share-alike sources is shipped in this dataset: no verbatim text from **ctext.org**, **zdic / 漢典**, or **pwxcoo** appears in `chengyu.json` (or in any derived seed output).
